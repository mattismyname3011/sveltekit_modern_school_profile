# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Prospective students and parents seeking information about programs, facilities, and school life.

## Product Purpose
A modern school profile and information hub to showcase academic excellence, programs, and community.

## Brand Personality
Informative, Professional, Vibrant.

## Anti-references
Generic corporate templates, cluttered designs with poor information hierarchy, low-contrast UI.

## Design Principles
- **Information First**: Prioritize clarity and ease of information discovery.
- **Visual Storytelling**: Use bold imagery and purposeful motion to engage.
- **Professional Polish**: High-quality typography and precise spacing to build trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, focus on readability and clear navigation.
